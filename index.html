🛡️ Stay Safe Online

Go get Dad 💛

You're not in trouble. Telling Dad is always the right move.

  1. Stop. Don't tap anything else.
  2. If something weird is on your screen, take a screenshot: press the side button and volume up together.
  3. Show Dad your phone. Right now is the best time, even if it feels awkward.

It's never too late to tell, even if it happened days ago.
